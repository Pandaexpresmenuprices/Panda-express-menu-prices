Panda Express Menu Prices: Complete Menu Guide for 2026

Compare bowls, plates, family meals, catering, nutrition, and rewards before you order.

Panda Express Menu Prices at a Glance

The fastest way to understand Panda Express food prices is to start with the meal format. A Bowl, Plate, and Bigger Plate each include a different number of entrées, while family meals and catering are priced for sharing.

  • Bowl: 1 side + 1 entrée, $9.20–$11.40
  • Plate: 1 side + 2 entrées, $11.10–$13.80
  • Bigger Plate: 1 side + 3 entrées, $12.80–$16.20
  • Panda Cub Meal: junior side + junior entrée + fruit + bottled water or kid's juice, $6.50–$11.20
  • Panda Bundles: Bowl, Plate, or Bigger Plate + medium fountain drink, $10.90–$17.45
  • 5 Person Family Meal: 2 large sides + 3 large entrées, $44–$60
  • À la carte items: $5–$19+
  • Drinks: $2.30–$4.50+

So, how much is Panda Express? For one person, a Bowl is usually the lowest-priced complete meal. A Plate gives you two entrées for a little more, while a Bigger Plate lets you try three dishes at once.

How Panda Express Pricing Works

When you look at a Panda Express price list, start with how much food you need and how many people you are feeding. A Bowl includes one side and one entrée, a Plate includes one side and two entrées, and a Bigger Plate includes one side and three entrées. À la carte ordering lets you buy individual entrées or sides separately.

Premium entrées, such as shrimp or steak dishes, may cost extra. Delivery fees, taxes, and promotions can also change the total, which is why similar meals can cost different amounts in different cities.

Panda Express Entrées, Sides, and Appetizers

The Panda Express full menu is built around wok-tossed entrées, with chicken, beef, shrimp, steak, and vegetable options. The Original Orange Chicken remains the most famous dish: crispy chicken in a sweet, tangy orange sauce. Other popular choices include Beijing Beef, Broccoli Beef, Grilled Teriyaki Chicken, Kung Pao Chicken, Mushroom Chicken, Honey Sesame Chicken Breast, and Honey Walnut Shrimp. Hot Orange Chicken offers the classic flavor with extra heat.

Sides complete the meal. The main choices are Chow Mein, Fried Rice, White Steamed Rice, and Super Greens, a lighter blend of broccoli, kale, and cabbage. A typical side costs about $4.50–$6.00 separately, depending on size and location.

Appetizers are useful for sharing. The menu includes Chicken Egg Rolls, Chicken Potstickers, Vegetable Spring Rolls, and Cream Cheese Rangoon. Drinks range from Coca-Cola and Barq's Root Beer to teas, lemonade, and specialty options such as Peach Lychee Refresher and Mango Guava Tea.

Family Meals, Bundles, and Catering

If you are feeding a group, compare total portions rather than individual prices. The 5 Person Family Meal includes 2 large sides and 3 large entrées. Panda Bundles add a medium fountain drink to a Bowl, Plate, or Bigger Plate, which can simplify ordering.

For larger events, Panda Express catering uses party-size trays. Party-size sides generally serve about 10–12 people, while party-size entrées serve about 12–14 people. Catering bundles cover groups of roughly 12–16, 18–22, and 26–30 people, with prices starting around $112 and rising above $276 for the largest bundle.

Nutrition, Rewards, and Ordering

Price is only part of the decision. Panda Express also publishes nutrition information to help you compare calories and protein. For example, Broccoli Beef has about 190 calories and 17 grams of protein, while Grilled Teriyaki Chicken has about 280 calories and 38 grams of protein. Pairing a lighter entrée with Super Greens can keep a meal lower in calories.

Panda Rewards members earn 10 Panda Points for every $1 spent on qualifying purchases, with rewards starting at 200 points. Members may also receive exclusive offers, a welcome gift, and a birthday gift, although offers change over time. You can order through pandaexpress.com, the app, in store, or for pickup or delivery where available. Hours are location-specific, although many standard locations operate from around 9:00–10:30 a.m. until 10:00–11:00 p.m.
